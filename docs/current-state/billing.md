# Platform billing

The operator (HomeX Media) invoicing the plumbing companies: invoices, payments, a receipt per payment, the PDFs and the emails. Plan: `docs/MULTI_TENANT_PLAN.md` §3.4 "Usage & billing" and decision #4 (flat monthly subscription per tenant).

These are the PLATFORM's books with its tenants, not a tenant's books with its customers. A plumber invoicing a homeowner is a different feature and does not exist yet.

### Where it lives
- Models (`bot/models.py`, migration 0094): `PlatformBillingProfile` (the issuer, one row, `current()`), `PlatformInvoice`, `PlatformInvoiceItem`, `PlatformPayment` (each payment IS a receipt).
- Screens: `bot/views/billing.py` under `/platform/billing/`, sidebar item **Billing** under Platform, mirrored in the mobile sheet. Templates `bot/pages/billing_*.html`, shared styles `bot/includes/billing_css.html`.
- PDFs: `bot/billing_pdf.py` (reportlab, returns bytes). Email: `bot/billing_emails.py`. The emblem is `bot/static/billing/homex_emblem.png` (cropped from `homex_logo.png`, the owner's 512px logo), read from disk.
- **Reachable from the admin panel and a phone** (owner, 2026-09-24): the platform console has a Billing button in its title row and an Invoice button on every tenant row (`billing_invoice_new?tenant=<slug>`); the sidebar item has its twin in the phone's More sheet, same superuser gate. `billing_css.html` carries a phone layer at <=767px: figures two to a row, 44px buttons, header buttons full width, invoice lines stacked as small cards (qty and unit price carry placeholders because the heading row is hidden), and every table in `pb-table-scroll`. Checked by screenshot at 390px; pinned by `BillingMobileTests`.
- Tests: `PlatformBillingTests`, `BillingReminderTests`, `BillingPdfTests` and `BillingMobileTests` in `bot/test_views_actions.py`.

### Rules
- **Superuser-only, on the VIEW.** Every billing view is `@superuser_required`; every state change is also `@require_POST`. Nothing reads `request.tenant`, so the operator's "View as" lens never narrows or widens what billing shows. A tenant-facing "your invoices" page would be a separate, tenant-scoped view.
- **Numbers are `HMX-<year>-<nnnn>` (invoices) and `HMX-R-<year>-<nnnn>` (receipts)** (owner chose the HMX prefix, 2026-09-24; `models.INVOICE_PREFIX` / `RECEIPT_PREFIX`), per year, from the highest number already issued (`_next_document_number`), with the unique index as the real guard and a retry on collision. A deleted draft leaves a gap, never a duplicate.
- **The issuer is SNAPSHOT onto each invoice** (`PlatformInvoice.issuer`) at creation. Editing Billing details changes invoices raised from then on; an old PDF downloaded again still shows the details it was issued with.
- **Status stored is only the operator's act: draft, sent, void.** Paid, part paid and overdue are derived (`display_status`) from the non-void payments and the due date, so a screen can never disagree with the receipts. Precedence: void, paid, part paid, overdue, sent, draft.
- **Only a draft can be edited or deleted.** Emailing it or "Mark as sent" issues it and locks the lines; a correction after that is void and reissue. A payment can be at most the balance (no overpayment, which would leave a negative balance with nowhere to go).
- **Void, never delete, once issued.** An invoice with standing receipts cannot be voided until those receipts are voided, so the books never show money received on a cancelled invoice. A voided receipt keeps its number and stops counting toward the paid figure.
- **Invoices outlive their tenant.** The tenant FK is `SET_NULL` (not the business tables' `PROTECT`), and `bill_to_*` is the snapshot the PDF prints. `platform_delete_tenant` needs no change for billing and does not delete invoices.
- **New invoice prefill:** the tenant's LAST invoice's bill-to, else the tenant's name (letterhead trading name first) and the internal-alerts inbox from their Profile page; this month as the period; one "Plumbot monthly subscription, <Month YYYY>" line at the default fee. The prefilled line is formset `initial` on GET only: built with the same initial on POST, Django would treat an untouched prefilled line as an empty extra form and drop it (`test_create_numbers_the_invoice_and_keeps_the_prefilled_line`).

### The document design (owner chose design C, "HomeX signature", 2026-09-24)
- Researched against the Stripe layout Anthropic and OpenAI send (title, number and dates first; a big "amount due" line; lines with the period under each; subtotal, total, amount due; a page footer) and NVIDIA-style enterprise billing (remit-to panel, terms). Three drafts were rendered with the logo; the owner picked C. Palette sampled from the logo: charcoal `#1A2225` (its background), electric blue `#0AA0F0` (its wordmark), cyan `#5FE3FF` (its glow).
- Layout: charcoal and blue band; the HX emblem on a rounded charcoal tile beside the name and tagline (the logo's own dark square is why it sits on a tile, not on white); a tinted amount panel with the dates on the right; From and Bill to; the lines; totals; a "How to pay" panel; a charcoal footer with the tagline, the billing email and "Page x of y" (`_NumberedCanvas`). Long invoices continue under a slim header.
- The panel says what matters now: Amount due; Balance due once part paid; Paid in full with a PAID stamp; "Overdue since" in red; VOID (red stamp) on a void invoice or receipt. How to pay is left off once nothing is owed. Receipts show THIS payment in the panel and the invoice's live standing in the totals.
- **Feature breakdown** (owner, 2026-09-24): the subscription line can print its cost allocated across Plumbot's features. The split lives in Billing details as "Feature | detail | percent" lines that must total 100 (`parse_breakdown`, refused on the page otherwise); default 40/15/15/10/10/10. Ticking Breakdown on a line allocates its total to the cent, remainder to the largest share (`allocate_breakdown`), and FREEZES it on `PlatformInvoiceItem.breakdown`, so a later change to the split never rewrites an issued invoice. It is informative: the line's amount, not the parts, is what is charged.
- **Currency per invoice** (owner: per tenant): US$ or R from a pick list; a tenant's next invoice starts in the currency of their last one.
- **Issuer details** (owner, 2026-09-24): "HomeX Media" only, no address or phone yet (a residential address is deliberately not printed), not VAT registered (will register with SARS; until then no VAT line and the title is INVOICE, not TAX INVOICE). How to pay starts as the FNB Business Zero account (`models.DEFAULT_PAYMENT_DETAILS`, with FNB's universal branch code 250655 and SWIFT FIRNZAJJ for payments from outside South Africa), editable in Billing details.
- The printed contact is the billing inbox (`PlatformBillingProfile.email`, default the operator's Gmail). billing@homexmedia.com cannot receive (no MX) until Cloudflare Email Routing is set up; after that, set the billing email to it and it prints instead.

### Email: platform mail, never Homebase's identity
- Sent with **`tenant=None`**. Passing the tenant would put an invoice behind that tenant's outbound-email switch (default OFF for all but Homebase) and send it from the tenant's own customer address.
- With `tenant=None`, the choke point's fallbacks (`DEFAULT_FROM_EMAIL`, `EMAIL_REPLY_TO`) default to **Homebase's own address**. So billing passes an explicit sender, `<issuer name> <PLATFORM_BILLING_FROM_EMAIL>` (default `billing@homexmedia.com`, the ROOT domain, by the owner's choice: an invoice is the business itself writing, not a notification. The root domain is authenticated in Brevo separately from `notifications.homexmedia.com` (its own brevo-code TXT, DKIM CNAMEs and DMARC in Cloudflare); until it is, Brevo refuses the sender and the send reports failed on the invoice page), and an explicit `reply_to` (the issuer's billing email). `send_email_to_recipients(reply_to=...)` exists for this; nothing else passes it. The issuer inbox is also Bcc'd, so the operator keeps a copy.
- **The billing inbox defaults to the operator's `PLATFORM_NOTIFICATION_EMAIL`** (jones86xi@gmail.com), set by `PlatformBillingProfile.current()` on first read only, so Reply-To and the Bcc copy reach a real inbox before Billing details is ever opened.
- Recorded as `SentEmail` category `billing` with no tenant. **The bot never answers billing mail** in that (polled) inbox: our Bcc copy is dropped by the `billing@` sender hint, and a tenant's reply by the `invoice inv-` / `receipt rct-` subject hints in `process_inbound_emails._AUTOMATED_SUBJECT_HINTS`. The subject guard matters because an owner's address can also sit on one of their own WhatsApp leads (an owner testing their bot), which would otherwise get a billing question answered as a plumbing enquiry. Pinned by `test_the_inbox_reader_never_answers_billing_mail`. Keep the subject formats in `billing_emails.py` in step with those hints.
- **DNS (verified 2026-09-24):** homexmedia.com is authenticated in Brevo with the `brevo-code` TXT on `@`, DKIM CNAMEs `brevo1/brevo2._domainkey` (DNS only, not proxied) and `_dmarc` `p=none`. The root has no MX, so billing@ cannot receive; replies rely on Reply-To.
- Manual sends, so the automated-email send windows do not apply. Copy follows the house rules even though the reader is a business: short lines, no emojis, no dash punctuation. Pinned by `test_email_goes_as_platform_mail_never_as_homebase`.

### Automatic reminders and the not-paid switch-off (owner, 2026-09-24; `bot/billing_reminders.py`)
- **Before due:** the tenant is emailed (invoice PDF attached, days left in the subject) at each "days before" in Billing details (default `14,7`) and on the due day. Each invoice can switch reminders off or set its own days on its page. Blank means the default.
- **Due day:** the operator gets "Has <tenant> paid <INV>?" with a link to the invoice page. From the due day that page shows the payment check: **They've paid** (jumps to Record a payment, which issues the receipt), **7 more days**, **N more days** (1 to 90), **Not paid**.
- **More days** moves the due date to the later of the old due date and today, plus N, and cancels any not-paid countdown. The log keys carry the date (`remind:<due>:<days>`), so the new date gets a fresh cycle.
- **Not paid** (only once due) sets `switch_off_on` = the later of due and today, plus 8. The tenant then gets "your Plumbot service will be switched off" at 7, 5, 1 and 0 days before it: the day after due, 3 days after, the day before, and on the day. On the day the operator gets a pause prompt linking to the page. **Nothing is switched off automatically:** the operator presses **Pause their account**, which sets `Tenant.is_active=False` (the console's Deactivate switch; Homebase refused) and offers **Switch their account back on** afterwards.
- **Pausing stops the webhook answering the tenant's customers and locks their staff out** (middleware drops inactive memberships). **It does NOT yet stop the scheduled crons** (`send_followups`, `send_reminders`, the nudge loops): none of them filter on `tenant.is_active`, so a paused tenant's leads can still receive follow-ups and reminders. That is the next thing to fix before a pause is a real switch-off.
- **Everything stops the moment the balance is 0 or the invoice is void** (`is_chaseable`). A draft is never chased.
- **Catch-up sends only the latest due step, one at a time**, and never a step scheduled before the invoice went out (or before Not paid was pressed), so a dead cron or a late invoice never produces a burst. A failed send is retried on later ticks at most `MAX_TRIES` (3) times.
- **Sent from 07:00 local** (`PlatformBillingProfile.reminder_time`, editable). The customer email send windows (12:30 to 13:30, 18:00 to 19:30) are for lead email and deliberately do not apply to billing mail to a business; the owner asked for 7AM.
- **Runs inside `send_scheduled_followups`** (the Email_Follow_Ups cron, every 5 minutes) in its own try, like the call-brief reminder, so no Railway service or `PLUMBOT_CRON` change is needed. `python manage.py send_billing_reminders --dry-run` lists what is due. `plan_for(invoice, today)` is the pure schedule, so the timeline tests send nothing.
- Every tenant subject contains "invoice HMX-..." (receipts "Receipt HMX-R-...") so a reply is left for the operator by the inbox reader's `invoice hmx-` / `receipt hmx-r-` hints; the first numbering's `invoice inv-` / `receipt rct-` stay in the list. **Change a prefix, change those hints.** Operator mail is from billing@, which the reader skips.
- Pinned by `BillingReminderTests` (day-by-day timelines for both phases, catch-up, the send time, retries, every stop condition, the page actions and the wording).
