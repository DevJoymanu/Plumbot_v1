# A quote for a WHOLE JOB must not be answered with a price sheet.
# Prod, barmak 2026-09-05: "May I get a quote for a four bedroomed house..."
# came back as "Our Facebook package is US$900, a freestanding tub and shower
# cubicle. If you're looking at just a tub..." — a bathroom price list for a
# whole-house job, and prices nobody asked for.
#
# Cause: the quote rule (a quote is delivered at the visit, never as a chat
# price block) lived only inside STEP 2, gated on the classifier landing on a
# priceable intent. A whole-job request names no product family, so it reached
# STEP 3, where the word "quote" reads as a pricing objection and fires the
# full pricing overview. Fixed by _routes_to_onsite_quote, asked before both
# pricing gates.

> May I get a quote for a four bedroomed house and 2 cottages
expect: all-in figure
expect: what area are you in
reject: US$
reject: package
reject: tub

> We are in Borrowdale
reject: US$
reject: package
