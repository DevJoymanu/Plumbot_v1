{% comment %} Attaching a quote to a lead, in one box. Before this the only way to raise a quote for a lead was to find them in the lead inbox, open them, and start the quote from their page. The quote screens themselves had no way to say who the quote is for, even though the search API behind this box already existed and was written for exactly this. So a plumber who opened "New Quote" had to leave it again to get the customer onto it. Tapping the box shows the most recent leads straight away, so the common case (the person they just spoke to) takes no typing at all. Typing filters by name, number, area, email or job type. The page it sits on defines `window.quoteLeadPicked(lead)`. This include knows how to FIND a lead, never what a particular sheet does with one, so the flat and sectioned editors share it without sharing their fields. Optional context: `quote_lead` is the lead already attached, if any. {% endcomment %}
