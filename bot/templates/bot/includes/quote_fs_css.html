{% comment %} The "fix and supply" quote sheet (letterhead.layout = "fix_and_supply"). WHAT: the look of the paper quote Homebase writes by hand - a three-column letterhead (logo | company name | contacts), "Quotation" centred and underlined, Client Name with the date on the right, a grey-ruled item table led by DESCRIPTION with item groups split by blank rows, a yellow-highlighted Material cost / Labour / Transport cost / Total fix and supply block, a bank details + "Fix and Supply | Total | figure" box, the tagline at the foot and a faint diagonal watermark. WHY an overlay and not a new sheet: the fix-and-supply sheet rides the grouped ("sectioned") editor, view and builder (quote_layout.SHEET_LAYOUTS), so these rules sit ON TOP of quote_sectioned_css.html under .bq-sheet--fs and change only what the paper shows differently. Include it AFTER the sectioned CSS, in the extra_css block, only when lh.layout == "fix_and_supply". HOW: .bq-sheet--fs is set on the sheet by the page; the section heading row becomes the blank separator (its title is the plumber's own grouping label and is never printed), the per-section SUB-TOTAL row is hidden (the paper has none), and rows marked .bq-fs-off (discount, SUB-TOTAL, VAT) are hidden because the paper has none either - their inputs stay in the form, so a figure saved before keeps its value. Column order is fixed by quote_fs_script.html. Mirrored by the fix-and-supply renderer in bot/quote_pdf.py (_fix_supply_sheet). {% endcomment %}
