{% comment %} The "start from a saved template" picker, shared by both quote layouts. The flat editor carried this markup inline and the sectioned sheet had no way to load a template at all, which meant a business on their own sheet had to retype the same section of items on every quote. One copy, one set of ids, so the two editors cannot drift into two different pickers. The page supplies the JS: it opens the modal, fills #templatesList, and decides what applying a template does to its own item rows. Expects `quote_project_types`. {% endcomment %}
